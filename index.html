About

Sheikh Jikrullah

I’m Sheikh Jikrullah, a Mumbai-based videographer, video editor and story writer. I believe a good video doesn’t begin on the editing timeline — it begins with a clear idea, a story worth telling and a purpose for the audience. During 2+ years at RKC Digital Media, I edited 500+ reels and videos, alongside my freelance work.

I’ve independently shot 100+ videos across real estate, finance, cafés and F&B, salons, clinics, brand promotions and social content. I can join at one stage or help carry a project from concept and script through production and final edit.
